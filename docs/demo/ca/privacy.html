Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Example Auto Inc. ("we", "us") buys used cars. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you ask us for an offer on your car at https://buy.example.com, why, how we protect it, and the choices you have. We follow the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where they apply, provincial privacy laws.

Our privacy contact is responsible for this policy:

  • Email: privacy@example.com
  • Phone: (416) 555-0100
  • Mail: 100 Example St, Toronto, ON M5V 2T6

Information you give us

When you fill in the form, you tell us:

  • About your car: make, model, year, mileage, condition, accident history, outstanding loan or lien, the price you have in mind, its VIN, the city and postal code where it is, and photos of it. Questions marked as optional can be skipped.
  • About you: your name and phone number, and, if you choose to give them, your email address and how you prefer to be contacted.

Before we store a photo, we remove the hidden information phones attach to it, including the location where it was taken.

We don't ask for your driver's licence, Social Insurance Number, bank details or home address. Please don't include them in your answers.

Information collected automatically

  • How you found us. If you reached the form by scanning our business card or through a labelled link, we record that label (for example "business card") with your inquiry, so we know which of our ads work.
  • A draft on your device. Until you submit, your answers are saved in your browser on your own device, so you can come back and finish within 7 days. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes the draft.
  • Your IP address is used briefly, in memory, to block abuse such as automated spam. We don't store it.

We don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.

Why we use it, and your consent

By sending the form and ticking the consent box, you agree that we use your information for these purposes only:

  • to evaluate your car and make you an offer;
  • to contact you about this inquiry by phone, text message or WhatsApp, or by email if you chose it;
  • to keep records of the cars we buy and to see which ways of finding us work.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us; we will then stop contacting you and delete your details, except what the law requires us to keep. We don't sell your personal information and don't use it for targeted advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Our hosting providers run the servers and the storage where your inquiry is kept. They process it on our behalf, under contract, and may not use it for their own purposes. Some of them may store it outside Canada, for example in the United States, where it may be accessible to courts and authorities under the laws there.
  • Push notification services. When you send an inquiry, we are notified on our phone through the notification service of the phone's maker (for example Apple or Google). The notification contains only the car and the price, never your name or contact details.
  • Messaging apps. If you and we talk through an app such as WhatsApp, that conversation is also covered by the app's own privacy policy.
  • We may disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your name, phone number, email address and the photos of your car while we work on your inquiry. 90 days after the inquiry is closed, whether we bought the car or not, they are erased automatically. We keep the other details of the car and the outcome, which no longer identify you, for our records. If we buy your car, the bill of sale and our accounting records are kept separately for as long as the law requires.

Some copies are out of reach of that automatic erasure, and we would rather say so plainly:

  • backups of our database, which are kept for a limited time and then deleted on a rolling basis;
  • calls, text messages and chats between you and us, which stay in the phones and messaging apps on both sides;
  • notifications already shown on our phone.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you and how we have used it, and ask us to correct it. You can also ask us to delete it or to stop contacting you. If we are in the middle of a deal with you, we will check with you before deleting what we need to finish it. We will answer within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (www.priv.gc.ca) or, where it applies, to your provincial privacy commissioner.

Children

This service is for adults selling a vehicle. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

Your information travels over encrypted connections, is stored on servers that only our application can reach, and is visible only to us after signing in.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date at the top.
