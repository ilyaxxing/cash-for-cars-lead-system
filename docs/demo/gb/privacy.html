Privacy Notice

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who we are

Example Motors Ltd ("we", "us") buys used cars and is the controller of the personal data you give us when you ask for an offer at https://buy.example.com. This notice explains what we collect, why, on what lawful basis, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

You can reach us at:

  • Email: privacy@example.com
  • Phone: 07700 900100
  • Post: 1 Example Street, Manchester M1 1AE

What you tell us

When you fill in the form, you tell us:

  • About your car: make, model, year, mileage, condition, accident history, outstanding finance, the price you have in mind, its registration number, the town and postcode where it is, and photos of it. Questions marked as optional can be skipped.
  • About you: your name and phone number, and, if you choose to give them, your email address and how you prefer to be contacted.

Before we store a photo, we remove the hidden information phones attach to it, including the location where it was taken.

We don't ask for your driving licence, National Insurance number, bank details or home address. Please don't include them in your answers.

What is collected automatically

  • How you found us. If you reached the form by scanning our business card or through a labelled link, we record that label (for example "business card") with your enquiry, so we know which of our adverts work.
  • A draft on your device. Until you submit, your answers are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device, so you can come back and finish within 7 days. It is needed for the service you asked for and is not used to track you. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes the draft.
  • Your IP address is used briefly, in memory, to block abuse such as automated spam. We don't store it.

We don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To value your car, make you an offer and contact you about this enquiry by phone, text message, WhatsApp or email: at your request, before a possible contract (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR), and with your consent to be contacted (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To see which ways of finding us work and to prevent abuse: our legitimate interests in running our business and protecting the service (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet legal obligations if we buy your car, such as keeping accounting records (Article 6(1)(c)).

We don't sell your personal data or use it for targeted advertising. We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Who else handles it

  • Our hosting providers run the servers and the storage where your enquiry is kept. They are processors: they act on our instructions, under contract (Article 28), and may not use the data for their own purposes. Some of them may be outside the UK, for example in the United States; where they are, the transfer is covered by the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.
  • Push notification services. When you send an enquiry, we are notified on our phone through the notification service of the phone's maker (for example Apple or Google). The notification contains only the car and the price, never your name or contact details.
  • Messaging apps. If you and we talk through an app such as WhatsApp, that conversation is also covered by the app's own privacy policy.
  • We may disclose data when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your name, phone number, email address and the photos of your car while we work on your enquiry. 90 days after the enquiry is closed, whether we bought the car or not, they are erased automatically. We keep the other details of the car and the outcome, which no longer identify you, for our records. If we buy your car, the sale paperwork and our accounts are kept separately for as long as the law requires.

Some copies are out of reach of that automatic erasure, and we would rather say so plainly:

  • backups of our database, which are kept for a limited time and then deleted on a rolling basis;
  • calls, text messages and chats between you and us, which stay in the phones and messaging apps on both sides;
  • notifications already shown on our phone.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to erase it, to restrict how we use it, or to send it to you or another company in a common format. You can object to our use of it based on legitimate interests, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting what we did before. If we are in the middle of a deal with you, we will check with you before erasing what we need to finish it.

To use any of these rights, contact us at privacy@example.com or by post at 1 Example Street, Manchester M1 1AE. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

This service is for adults selling a vehicle. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Security

Your data travels over encrypted connections, is stored on servers that only our application can reach, and is visible only to us after signing in.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date at the top.
