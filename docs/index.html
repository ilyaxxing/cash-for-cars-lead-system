The setup

Paste one line. Claude asks, you answer.

Open Claude Code (for example the Code tab in the Claude desktop app) in an empty folder and send:

Set up https://github.com/ilyaxxing/cash-for-cars-lead-system for my client.
  1. One line in Claude Code. It starts everything below.
  2. Questions about the business. Its name, phone, look and what it buys.
  3. Deploy. Claude builds it on Railway and connects the business's domain.
  4. A test lead. You send a car through the form; it never counts in the statistics.
  5. The owner's phone. The app on the home screen, notifications on, a test push.
  6. Card and QR codes. The print-ready card, and a QR code or link for every other place.
  7. Hand-over. The project moves to the business's own Railway account.

About an hour for the setup itself once the accounts are ready; the hand-over and the domain's DNS can take up to a day. Claude tells you when a step needs a person: signing in, payment, the domain's DNS settings.

Requirements and limits

What it takes, and what isn't proven yet.

You

  • Claude Code, which needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher) or an Anthropic API account
  • A Mac or a Linux computer; on Windows, WSL
  • Your own Railway account on the Hobby plan, from $5 a month: each project is built there before it is handed over

The business

  • Its own Railway account with its card, hosting from $5 a month; the finished project is handed over to it
  • Its domain, or a new one in its name (about $10–15 a year)
  • Its name, email, and the owner's phone for ten minutes

In Russia

The business needs its own server in the country, its own domain and a lawyer's check of the privacy policy. Installing on such a server has so far been tested only on a local virtual machine, not with a public domain. See Another country.

The system is tested end to end for the US. The prices and the hand-over to a client's own Railway account have not been tried with a real client yet. All known limits are under Limitations in the README.
