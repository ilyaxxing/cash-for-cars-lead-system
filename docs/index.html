The business

Sell it once, set it up in an afternoon.

Cash-for-cars buyers leave cards under windshield wipers. Many people who would never call a stranger will scan a code. You install this system for the buyer, under their name, on their own account.

01

Find the buyers

Every "We buy cars" card under your wiper is one. So are the numbers on road signs and the "we buy cars" listings on maps. They already spend money on finding sellers.

02

Charge for the setup

$300–600 for a turnkey setup, plus an optional support plan at $25–50 a month. Hosting (about $5 a month (Railway Hobby, includes $5 of usage; not yet checked against a real client's bill)) and the domain (about $10–15 a year) are on the client's own accounts.

03

Install with Claude

Claude Code asks the questions, deploys, connects the owner's phone and makes the print-ready card, plus a QR code or link for stickers, partners' posters, signs and map listings. About an hour once the accounts and the domain are ready.

Prices, who buys it and the other niches it fits are in the business doc. Suggested starting points, not market-tested yet.
